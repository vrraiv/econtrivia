## Format

```
## [proposed-id] | category | difficulty
```

### 2025 and 2026 Nobel Economics Prize
**Issue:** Nobel announcements fall in October. Knowledge cutoff is August 2025, so the 2025
and 2026 prizes are unknown. Search before adding any question about these laureates.

### Canada merchandise export share to US
**Prompt:** "What percentage of Canada's total merchandise exports went to the United States in 2022?"
**Issue:** Exact Statistics Canada figure not confirmed in this session. Roughly 75–77% by most
accounts, but the precise StatCan number should be verified before writing the question.
Source to check: https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/71-607-x/2018011/cte-tce-eng.htm

### First stock exchange
**Prompt:** "The Amsterdam Stock Exchange, founded in 1602 for the Dutch East India Company,
is generally considered the world's first modern stock exchange. True or false?"
**Issue:** Some historians credit earlier institutions in Antwerp or Bruges. Phrase carefully
as "generally considered" or skip until a cleaner source is confirmed.

### Canada's Harmonized Sales Tax rate in Ontario
**Prompt:** "What is the combined federal and provincial Harmonized Sales Tax (HST) rate in Ontario?"
**Answer candidate:** 13% (5% federal GST + 8% provincial portion)
**Issue:** Rate confirmed as correct since 2010, but double-check with CRA before including,
especially if the question bank is updated after potential future rate changes.

### GDP per capita ranking
Any question about Canada's GDP per capita ranking relative to peer countries — these shift
annually and a specific rank is hard to nail down to a stable year without a precise citation.
